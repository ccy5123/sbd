## Why each fits (recent work)
1. **Licina** made the boundary-layer and intake-fraction measurements the CFD is validated against (Licina 2014,
   2015, 2017); recent: Build. Environ. 2026 doi:10.1016/j.buildenv.2026.115279, Environ. Sci. Technol. 2024
   doi:10.1021/acs.est.3c08466. Heavily cited, and the manuscript reports its plume 40-56 % faster and its intake
   fraction above his data: a demanding but fair reviewer, not a conflict.
2. **Năstase**: particle dynamics in the human thermal plume (Build. Environ. 2026 doi:10.1016/j.buildenv.2026.115058),
   review of CFD validation for indoor environments (Indoor Air 2025 doi:10.1155/ina/6601284).
3. **Ghaddar**: microenvironment and personal-exposure CFD (Indoor Environments 2026 doi:10.1016/j.indenv.2026.100196).
4. **Burridge**: exposure uncertainty from point measurements in this journal (J. R. Soc. Interface 2024
   doi:10.1098/rsif.2024.0270); buoyancy-driven room flows (Flow 2023 doi:10.1017/flo.2023.11).
5. **Diwan**: aerosol puffs between speakers (Flow 2022 doi:10.1017/flo.2022.7); heated turbulent jets (Int. J. Heat
   Fluid Flow 2023 doi:10.1016/j.ijheatfluidflow.2023.109190).
6. **Oleszkiewicz**: olfactory perception across 16 regions (iScience 2025 doi:10.1016/j.isci.2025.113455); air
   pollution and smell (Sci. Rep. 2024 doi:10.1038/s41598-024-75067-z). The manuscript cites Stefańczyk &
   Oleszkiewicz 2020 (not a conflict).
7. **Alves**: social amplification of illusory correlations (Eur. J. Soc. Psychol. 2024 doi:10.1002/ejsp.3104);
   sampling-based judgement (Psychol. Rev. 2026 doi:10.1037/rev0000647).
8. **Yang**: excretion and bird dust-bathing mechanics (PNAS 2026 doi:10.1073/pnas.2521949123; Soft Matter 2022
   doi:10.1039/d2sm00359g). Former PhD student of David Hu (see below).

## Avoid or handle with care
- **Klaus Fiedler** (cited): died May 2026. Exclude.
- **David L. Hu** (Georgia Tech; senior author of Ancalle et al. 2021, named in the cover letter as the closest prior
  work): his group has an unpublished physics-of-flatulence project, so he may be a competitor. Neither suggest nor
  oppose without a concrete reason. If he is a concern, Yang (his former student) may also be better left out.
- Older cited authors (Levitt, Suarez, Nazaroff, Melikov, Hudson) were not checked and may be retired.
- No one needs to be opposed on the evidence found.
