\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A silent passage of flatus is not deadlier than an audible one.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Per molecule it is delivered somewhat more efficiently, mostly by too little to notice.
  \item As actually emitted, it wins half the time.
  \item On a sofa it may reach no one but the ceiling.
\end{itemize}
The belief that it is deadlier is real, widespread and old, and it follows from how memory samples events rather than
from how odour travels. The nose was fooled by the ears.
