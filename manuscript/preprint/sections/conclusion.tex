\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\begin{itemize}
  \item At equal mass, a silent release reaches a neighbour's nose at a median 1.45 times the concentration of an
  audible one, often below the just-noticeable ratio.
  \item As emitted, a silent passage is the stronger one at the neighbour in 48\% of pairs in the analytic model, and
  at a bystander in 21\% (0--70\%) of pairs in the simulated scenes.
  \item On a sofa the silent release rises to the ceiling and reaches neither nose within 30\,s. In an elevator with
  the releaser in the front row, it is strongest for the person behind, who also smells it first.
  \item A silent passage is noticed only if smelled; in literature observer models this selection makes a remembered
  silent passage seem the stronger one in 68--93\% of comparisons.
\end{itemize}
