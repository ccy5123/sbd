\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\gap{Why the belief exists.} Selection is the main route \cite{berkson1946,snoep2014}; amplifiers are distinctiveness
\cite{hamilton1976}, availability \cite{tversky1973} and sampling \cite{fiedler2000,denrell2005}; transmission of the
phrase itself is the competing route \cite{chapman1967}.
\begin{figure}
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{fig12_culture_map.png}
\caption{\gap{caption} The belief across languages, by evidence level.}
\label{fig:culture}
\end{figure}

\gap{The emitter.} Own flatus is rated less disgusting \cite{stevenson2005,liuzza2016}; belief about the source shifts
hedonic ratings \cite{hold1977,mcburney1976}; no study compares real own and other flatus.

\gap{Predictions.} Emitters hold the belief less than bystanders; anosmic people never notice silent passages; masking
effects are ambiguous.

\gap{When a silent release really is worse:} bare, still air, the releaser's own nose, within about 5\,s.

\gap{Limitations.} RANS and a single realisation; 30\,s window; body-plume speed over-predicted by 40--56\%; no human
measurements; base rate unmeasured; one composition dataset \cite{suarez1997,suarez1998}.
